# Needlewood V6: Farm tycoon version (from the owner's brief)

## Decisions
- Spawn at a shared base camp with starter hay barrels, a basic shovel and a wheelbarrow.
- Shared hub, private plot: your barrels, plot and mill are private; the hub is shared and used for trading.
- One open world with roads: drive a truck between barrel maps (different hay and needle types per map).
- Mythic item (alien collector) via a cryptic multi-step quest, with one rotating element.
- Needles: every scoop of hay hides needles. The basic shop pays ~$1 per needle in a raw load. Once you can sort, needles are collected and sold in groups/bundles at ~$100 each (placeholder). Richer biomes hold more and different needles, plus collectible foreign objects. Special needles are building materials (e.g. sand needles -> sand blocks) for building a farm like Lumber Tycoon 2.

## Core loop
Scoop -> haul -> sell raw -> store in mill -> sort -> bundle and sell -> build.

## Systems
- Plot of land bought at the hub; first building is the mill (stores hay, feeds sorter).
- Sorter tiers (recovery / speed, placeholders): hand sieve 15%, shaker table 35%, conveyor sorter 55%, magnet drum 75%, optical sorter 92%, alien collector 100% (instant). Unrecovered needles are lost in the hay.
- Trucks only (pickup, flatbed, box, heavy, semi) with trailers (wagon, small, medium, large, double). Mill and silo upgrades raise storage.
- Tools: shovel -> rake -> better; mythic alien collector empties one haystack of needles (once per day).
- Alien ritual: crop circle clue at night; find three foreign objects; lay hay bales in a daily-changing shape at your plot at midnight; light takes you up.
- Needle materials by map: Steel (steel plates), Pine (needle planks), Sand (sand bricks), Ice (ice glass), Bog (mossy stone), Obsidian (black glass). Foreign objects go in a collection book.

## Risks
- Raw -> sorted jump is huge (about 15-30x per load with placeholder numbers); keep hay useful (building and ritual input).
- Scoop/haul can get dull; put polish, combos and glints at the sorter.
- Needle building is large; ship after the sorter.
