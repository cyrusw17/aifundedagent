## Changes from V1
- One connected island (Meadow Farm, Pine Forest, Snow Peak, Haunted Grove, Homestead Valley, Volcano, Desert Dunes, Swamp, The Spindle, plus Cave, Sky Island, Sunken Harbor).
- Three loops: Harvest (lumber), Hunt (needles), Process (hauled bales at base).
- Layered mystery: the Weaver vanished; the Master Needle broke into 12 shards, one per region. Tailor NPC hints, golden threads, stitch glyphs on needle eyes, world timers (lighthouse, tide, meteor shower), server-wide goals, seasonal chapters.
- Artifact codex (12 shown, 50+ planned): Weaver's Thimble, Golden Shears, Scarab Thimble, Magma Spindle, Silver Bobbin, Thread of Fate, Frozen Bobbin, Pincushion Heart, Moth-Eaten Map, Cracked Compass, Lens of the Lighthouse, Meteorite Needle.
- Events: Meteor Shower, Eclipse, Hay Fever, Low Tide, Thread Storm, Hay Golem boss.
- Systems: vehicles, base + artifact museum, pets, bounty board, clans, trading with 24h lock, seasons.

## Phases
1. Slice: Meadow Farm, hay Stack, 3 tools, 5 needles, Tailor NPC
2. Lumber: Pine Forest, sawmill, base machine line, trailers
3. Island: connected map, roads, Snow Peak, 3 secrets, 6 artifacts
4. Mystery: thread, glyphs, journal pages, lighthouse, server goal
5. Expand: a biome + 4 artifacts every ~3 weeks, events, pets, clans, seasons

## Risks
Scope (cut regions, never the three loops); mystery solved too fast (gate behind timers/server goals); map streaming (StreamingEnabled + zoning); exploits (server-authoritative finds and trades).

Wood, region and artifact names are original to this project (not Lumber Tycoon 2 names).

## Archipelago update: sailing and the ocean highway
- **Thimble Keys** (lv 35-55, Coralwood, Conch Sunhat, Pearl Needle): reached by the **Longstitch Highway**, a bridge from the Desert coast with a dark undersea tunnel, a toll gate (paid in planks) and a Midway Rest Stop. Any wheeled vehicle works, including a semi with a trailer. Storms flood road sections for 10 minutes, so boats are the backup.
- **Bobbin Rock** (lv 45-60, Brinewood, Captain's Tricorn, Anchor Needle): boat only, via Ash Dock past the Graveyard of Ships (reef wrecks, night Kraken). Holds the Weaver's lost workshop and the 12th shard.
- Boats: Rowboat (lake/coast, 2 bales), Sailboat (ocean, wind, 10 bales, required for Bobbin Rock), Speedboat (fast, fuel, 6 bales), Cargo barge (carries a full flatbed trailer).
- New secrets: Longstitch Toll Gate, Midway Rest Stop, Undersea Tunnel, Ash Dock, Swamp Port, Graveyard of Ships.
