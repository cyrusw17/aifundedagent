## Five real problems
1. Dig fun is untested. 2. Scope is three games. 3. First ten minutes undefined. 4. Mystery will be wikied in a week. 5. No economy or phone plan.

## Improvements
- P0: pitch "chop to haul, haul to sift, sift to find"; logs and bales share trailers, trucks and dump pit. Prototype the dig first (probe, sift, pull; keep only if most testers keep digging after 5 min). Script the first 10 minutes (guaranteed first needle at ~2:00, first trailer ~6:00, gold-thread hook ~8:00, base plot ~10:00). Cut launch to the main island, 4 biomes, no boats/highway.
- P1: phone-first controls; idle base line (up to 8h offline, longer cap as gamepass); half-fixed/half-rotating secrets (seeded by day/server, shuffled journal pages); economy with sinks (trade tax, fuel/repair, tolls; only mutations and non-core artifacts tradeable); no paid random rewards (Roblox requires odds disclosure; needles are earned, never sold).
- P2: co-op (two-seat trucks, trailer trains, Hay Golem, clan digs); telemetry from day one.

## Scope ladder
MFP (4 wk): Meadow + dig. Launch (+10 wk): 4 biomes, shared logistics, base idle line, trailers, 3 secrets, 12 artifacts, trading. Update 1: Spindle + mystery. Update 2: boats + Thimble Keys by ocean highway. Update 3: Bobbin Rock, Kraken, season one finale.

## Stop/adjust checks (targets to test)
Testers digging after 5 min; time to first needle under 3 min; next-day return at least a third; most 10-minute players reach a base plot; near-zero scam/dupe trades.
