# Named ICT Strategies & Concepts Cheatsheet

## Daily Bias
Decide expected delivery of the day **before** hunting entries.
Inputs:
- Higher-timeframe structure
- Relative position in dealing range (premium/discount)
- Nearest draws: PDH/PDL, equal H/L, HTF FVG, weekly extremes
- Overnight Asia range context

Bullish bias → hunt SSL raids → longs toward BSL  
Bearish bias → hunt BSL raids → shorts toward SSL

## Power of 3 (AMD)
Accumulation → Manipulation → Distribution. Frame the daily candle as intentional delivery, not random OHLC.

## Silver Bullet
Time-based FVG scalp model in fixed one-hour windows (London / NY AM / NY PM). Requires liquidity + displacement FVG inside the hour.

## Turtle Soup
Fade of a failed run on stops beyond a known high/low. High synergy with MSS + breaker.

## OTE Model
Enter impulse retracements into 62–79% when they overlap PD arrays in the correct half of the range.

## 2022 Mentorship-style model (community shorthand)
Often summarized as:
- HTF bias
- Liquidity sweep
- Market structure shift
- FVG entry
Used widely as the “simplified ICT” backbone that SMC/TJR educators operationalize.

## SMT Divergence
Divergence between correlated markets (e.g., ES vs NQ, EURUSD vs GBPUSD, DXY vs risk pairs).
- One makes a new extreme, the other fails → potential reversal / stronger confirmation of raid
Use as **confluence**, not a standalone system.

## IPDA / algorithmic delivery framing (ICT language)
ICT teaches price as algorithmically delivered between liquidity and inefficiencies across dealing ranges and time. Operational takeaway for funded trading: **think in draws, arrays, and time**, not indicator lag.
