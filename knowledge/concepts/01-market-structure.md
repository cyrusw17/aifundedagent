# Market Structure — BOS, CHoCH, MSS

Structure is the map. Without it, every OB/FVG is noise.

## Bullish structure
- Series of **Higher Highs (HH)** and **Higher Lows (HL)**
- Pullbacks hold above prior HL

## Bearish structure
- Series of **Lower Lows (LL)** and **Lower Highs (LH)**
- Rallies fail below prior LH

## Break of Structure (BOS)
- Price **breaks and closes** beyond the last protective swing in the trend direction
- Bullish: close above prior swing high → continuation
- Bearish: close below prior swing low → continuation
- Use BOS to **stay with** the trend and trail narrative

## Change of Character (CHoCH) / Market Structure Shift (MSS)
- Early evidence that the prior trend may be ending
- Bullish MSS: after a downtrend / SSL raid, price displaces through a meaningful LH
- Bearish MSS: after an uptrend / BSL raid, price displaces through a meaningful HL
- Prefer MSS that arrives **with displacement** (impulsive candles + FVG), not a weak poke

## External vs internal structure
- **External range liquidity (ERL)** — swing highs/lows that define the dealing range
- **Internal range liquidity (IRL)** — equal highs/lows, short-term swings, inducement *inside* the range
- Typical narrative: raid internal → reverse toward external (or vice versa depending on bias)

## Practical rules
1. Define swings on your **bias timeframe** first (often Daily / 4H / 1H)
2. Confirm shifts on **execution timeframe** (15m / 5m / 1m)
3. A “shift” without a prior **liquidity event** is lower quality
4. Do not call every tiny break an MSS — require meaningful swing + displacement
