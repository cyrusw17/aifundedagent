# Premium, Discount, OTE & PD Arrays

## Dealing range
Defined by a meaningful swing high and swing low.
- Midpoint (50%) = **equilibrium (EQ)**
- Above 50% = **premium** (expensive — favor shorts if bias bearish)
- Below 50% = **discount** (cheap — favor longs if bias bullish)

## Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)
Fibonacci retracement of the impulse leg, typically the **0.62–0.79** region (ICT often highlights ~0.705).
Best used as a **refinement** overlapping an OB/FVG inside discount (buys) or premium (sells).

## PD Arrays (Premium/Discount Arrays)
Institutional “tools” you expect price to react from, arranged by side of the range:

**Bullish arrays (seek in discount):**
- Bullish OB
- Bullish FVG / BISI
- Breaker (bullish context)
- Mitigation block
- Old low / rejection block variants (as taught in ICT PD matrix)

**Bearish arrays (seek in premium):**
- Bearish OB
- Bearish FVG / SIBI
- Breaker (bearish context)
- Mitigation block
- Old high variants

## Execution rule
Bias + draw on liquidity tells **direction**.  
PD array in the correct half of the range tells **where**.  
LTF confirmation tells **when**.
