# Order Blocks, Breakers & Mitigation

## Order Block (OB)
Last opposing candle before a displacing move that breaks structure.
- **Bullish OB**: last down-close (or bearish candle) before bullish displacement / BOS-MSS
- **Bearish OB**: last up-close before bearish displacement / BOS-MSS

Valid OB checklist:
1. Last opposing candle
2. Strong displacement after it
3. Structure break confirmation
4. Aligns with HTF bias and liquidity narrative
5. Clean invalidation beyond the block / sweep extreme

## Mitigation
Price returning into the OB/FVG to fill unfilled interest / rebalance. First tap is often highest quality; repeated taps weaken the level.

## Breaker Block
A failed order block that price traded through, then used in the opposite role.
- Classic: bullish OB fails → becomes bearish breaker on retest (and vice versa)
- High value when it coincides with MSS + liquidity raid

## Mitigation Block
Related idea: the candle/block left after a failure that becomes the new reaction zone. Different educators label slightly differently; focus on **failed level → role reversal → retest**.

## Practical tip
Do not mark every candle as an OB. Mark the one that **originated the displacement that changed or continued structure** after liquidity was taken.
