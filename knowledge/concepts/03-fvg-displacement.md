# Displacement & Fair Value Gaps (FVG)

## Displacement
Impulsive institutional delivery:
- Large-bodied candles
- Little opposing overlap
- Often creates BOS/MSS
- Leaves inefficiency (FVG)

Displacement is the **signature** that a structure break is meaningful.

## Fair Value Gap (3-candle imbalance)
Bullish FVG:
- Candle 1 high does not overlap candle 3 low
- Gap between candle 1 high and candle 3 low = bullish imbalance

Bearish FVG:
- Candle 1 low does not overlap candle 3 high
- Gap between candle 3 high and candle 1 low = bearish imbalance

Price often returns to “rebalance” before continuing.

## How traders use FVGs
1. **Entry magnet** after MSS (retrace into FVG)
2. **Continuation confluence** (price holds FVG and expands)
3. **Inverse FVG** — prior FVG fails / flips role; used by TJR-style traders as order-flow change evidence
4. **CE (consequent encroachment)** — midpoint of the FVG as refined entry

## Quality filters
- Prefer FVGs created by **displacement that broke structure**
- Prefer FVG in **correct premium/discount half** for the bias
- Fresh (unmitigated) > heavily traded through
- HTF FVG > LTF noise FVG

## Imbalance vs gap
On FX charts, FVG is a visual proxy for inefficient delivery. Treat it as a **zone**, not a single tick.
