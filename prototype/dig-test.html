Needlewood Dig Test

The review said the whole game rests on whether digging a hay pile feels good. This is that moment, playable. Press and drag across the hay. Watch the ring around your tool: blue is cold, red is hot.

What this is testing

Three beats

  • Probe. The ring and meter get hotter as you near a needle. Turn sound on for a rising hum.
  • Sift. Hay flies as you dig, deeper tools clear faster.
  • Pull. A light beam, sized by rarity, marks the find.

Questions for a tester

  • Do you want to keep digging after five minutes?
  • Did the ring tell you where to go without being told?
  • Was the first needle too easy, too slow, or about right?
  • Did the rake feel worth buying?

Not in this test

  • Trees, sawmill, trailers and base
  • Other biomes and the mystery
  • Trading and saving progress

Prototype for feel only. Prices, rarities and tool ranges are placeholders to tune from playtests. Design review: the Needlewood Design Review page.
