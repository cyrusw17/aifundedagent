What changed from the first test

New hooks to feel

  • Rarity tell. The sparkle takes the needle's color.
  • Near miss. SO CLOSE bobs over your tool.
  • Combo. Chain finds within 45 seconds for up to x2.5.
  • Luck. Common finds fill the bar. Full means the next find upgrades a tier.
  • Polish. Tap the dusty needle to reveal a mutation.

Questions for a tester

  • Do you want to keep digging after five minutes?
  • Did the ring tell you where to go without being told?
  • Was the first needle too easy, too slow, or about right?
  • Did the rake feel worth buying?

Also here

  • Bounties and an Almost there panel, so something is always nearly done.
  • An idle line that earns while you are away, up to 8 hours.
  • A daily streak that forgives one missed day.
  • A simulated server feed. The other players are fake.
